About the Institute

A legacy of quality technical & management education

Located in the educational hub of Knowledge Park-III, Greater Noida, KCC Institute of Technology & Management (KCCITM) is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow.

The institute blends a rigorous academic curriculum with hands-on learning, industry interaction, research, entrepreneurship and co-curricular activities so that every graduate is ready for industry, higher studies and life.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in technical and management education that nurtures competent, ethical and innovative professionals for society and industry.

Our Mission

To deliver outcome-based education through dedicated faculty, modern infrastructure and strong industry partnerships, fostering research, innovation, leadership and lifelong learning.

Admissions

Begin your journey at KCCITM

  1. Check eligibilityB.Tech: 10+2 with PCM (min 45%). MBA: Graduation (min 50%).
  2. Appear for entranceJEE Main / CUET for B.Tech · CUET-PG / CAT / MAT / CMAT for MBA.
  3. AKTU counselling or direct admissionChoose KCCITM during AKTU counselling or apply for management quota seats.
  4. Document verification & feeSubmit documents at the admission cell and pay the first semester fee.
🎖️ Merit scholarships, fee concessions for meritorious students and assistance with UP scholarship schemes are available.
