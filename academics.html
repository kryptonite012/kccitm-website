Teaching & Learning

How you’ll learn at KCC ITM

Learning mapped to outcomes

Each course is designed around clear Course Outcomes (COs) linked to Programme Outcomes (POs), in line with AICTE and NBA frameworks. Continuous assessment — quizzes, assignments, lab evaluations and sessional tests — complements AKTU end-semester examinations.

  • Defined course & programme outcomes
  • Continuous internal evaluation
  • Feedback-driven course improvement
📐
Outcome-based curriculumAligned with AKTU & AICTE model syllabus

Classroom to industry

Guest lectures, industrial visits, summer internships and value-added certification courses expose students to current technologies and workplace practices.

  • Expert talks & workshops
  • Mandatory summer training / internships
  • Value-added certification courses
🏭
Industry exposureVisits, internships & live projects

Build, test, publish

Mini-projects in the early years lead to a final-year major project. Students are encouraged to take part in hackathons, publish papers and file innovations through the Institution’s Innovation Council.

  • Mini & major projects
  • Hackathons and technical competitions
  • Research papers & IPR awareness
🔬
Research cultureFrom idea to prototype

Every student has a mentor

Faculty mentors track academic progress, attendance and wellbeing, and connect students with remedial classes, counselling and career guidance.

  • Faculty mentor–mentee system
  • Remedial & doubt-clearing classes
  • Regular parent communication
