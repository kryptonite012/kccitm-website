Residential Life

Hostel & transport

Comfortable, secure living

Furnished rooms on twin/triple sharing, a hygienic mess serving vegetarian meals, Wi-Fi, recreation room and wardens available round the clock.

  • 24×7 security & CCTV
  • Mess with weekly menu
  • RO drinking water & power back-up
  • Indoor games & common room

Connected across Delhi-NCR

College buses run on routes across Greater Noida, Noida, Ghaziabad and nearby areas. The campus is also accessible via the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway and Aqua Line metro connectivity in the region.

  • Multiple routes & pickup points
  • Trained drivers & route in-charges
  • Route-wise transport fee
