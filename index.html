About KCC ITM

Where ambition meets opportunity in India’s education hub

KCC Institute of Technology & Management was founded in 2009 in Greater Noida’s Knowledge Park — one of North India’s largest education clusters. The institute is approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow.

  • B.Tech in CSE and specialisations in AI & ML, Data Science, IoT and AI & DS
  • Core engineering branches, M.Tech and a dual-specialisation MBA
  • Corporate Resource Centre for training, internships and placements
  • Hostels, transport across Delhi-NCR, labs, library and active student clubs
