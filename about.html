Overview

A KCC Group institution in Knowledge Park III

KCC Institute of Technology & Management (KCC ITM) is a constituent of the KCC Group of Institutions, Greater Noida. Established in 2009, it is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow.

The institute offers B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering and its emerging specialisations — Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Data Science, Internet of Things, and AI & Data Science — along with core engineering branches, M.Tech and MBA.
