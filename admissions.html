How to Join

Admission process

Admissions follow AKTU / UPTAC counselling and Government of Uttar Pradesh norms.

Appear for entrance

JEE Main for B.Tech; CUET-PG / CAT / MAT / CMAT for MBA; GATE (preferred) for M.Tech.

Register for counselling

Register on the AKTU / UPTAC counselling portal and fill choices — include KCC ITM.

Seat allotment

Accept the allotted seat and pay the seat-confirmation fee as instructed by UPTAC.

Report to campus

Bring original documents for verification at the institute’s admission cell.

Begin your journey

Complete fee payment, hostel/transport registration and join the induction programme.

Direct admission: Any seats available for direct/institute-level admission are filled as per state government rules. Contact the admission cell at 0120-2320640 for current availability.

📁

Documents required

10th & 12th mark sheets, entrance scorecard, allotment letter, transfer & character certificates, migration certificate (if applicable), category / income / domicile certificates, Aadhaar, 6 passport photos.

🩺

Medical & undertakings

Medical fitness certificate, anti-ragging undertakings (student and parent) as per UGC/AICTE regulations, and gap certificate if applicable.

🗺️

Outstation students

Students from outside U.P. are welcome; hostel and transport registration can be completed on the day of reporting.
