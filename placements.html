Training Ladder

Four years of career preparation

1️⃣

Year 1 — Foundation

Communication skills, programming fundamentals, personality development and orientation to careers.

2️⃣

Year 2 — Skill building

Data structures, aptitude basics, group discussion practice and first technical certifications.

3️⃣

Year 3 — Industry readiness

Summer internships, advanced aptitude, domain training, mock interviews and resume building.

4️⃣

Year 4 — Placement

Company-specific preparation, pre-placement talks and campus / pool recruitment drives.

What the CRC offers

  • Aptitude, reasoning and verbal ability training
  • Coding bootcamps and technical interview preparation
  • Soft skills, group discussions and mock interviews
  • Internship and live-project facilitation
  • Pool campus drives with partner institutions
  • Higher-studies and entrepreneurship guidance
